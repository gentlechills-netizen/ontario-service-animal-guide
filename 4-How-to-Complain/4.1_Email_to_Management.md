# Email to Upper Management

*Use this template for a swift, documented follow-up after an access denial; or in combination with the "Formal Letter" template*

---

**Subject:** URGENT: AODA Violation / Denial of Access for a Service Animal User at [ **Store/Location Name** ]

**To the Management of [ Business Name ],**

I am writing to formally report a violation of the _Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act_ (AODA) and the Ontario Human Rights Code that occurred at your establishment. 

On [ **Date** ] at approximately [ **Time** ], I attempted to access your location at [ **Address** ] accompanied by my Service Animal. 

**[ SELECT THE CIRCUMSTANCE THAT APPLIES AND DELETE THE OTHERS: ]**

> **[ Option A: Denied Entry Completely ]**
> I was explicitly denied entry by [ **Name/Description of Employee** ], who stated that animals were not allowed on the premises, disregarding my rights as a Service Animal User.

> **[ Option B: Asked for Invalid Certification ]**
> I was stopped by [ **Name/Description of Employee** ] who demanded a "training certificate" or "ID card." Under Ontario law, this documentation does not exist and cannot be legally required. I offered my valid Medical Letter from a regulated health professional, but it was dismissed.

> **[ Option C: Harassed / Questioned about Disability ]**
> I was granted access, but subjected to inappropriate and illegal questioning by [ **Name/Description of Employee** ] regarding my specific medical diagnosis and the tasks my animal performs, which is a violation of my medical privacy.

Under O. Reg. 165/16 s. 80.47 (2), obligated organizations must allow persons with disabilities to be accompanied by their Service Animal. A letter from a regulated health professional is the only legal proof required (O. Reg. 165/16 s. 80.45 4(b)).

I trust that you will treat this matter with the seriousness it deserves. Please review your AODA compliance protocols and ensure your staff are immediately retrained on Service Animal access rights. I request a written response outlining the steps you will take to rectify this situation by [ **Date - usually 7 days from now** ].
