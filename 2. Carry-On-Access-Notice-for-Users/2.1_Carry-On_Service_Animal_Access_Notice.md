# SERVICE ANIMAL ACCESS NOTICE
**To Business Owners, Managers, and Security Personnel:**

This dog, **[Insert Name of Dog/Animal Here]**, is a legally recognized Service Animal under the **Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA)** and the **Ontario Human Rights Code (OHRC)**.

Under provincial law, [**obligated organizations**](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/r16165) **(O. Reg. 165/16 s. 80.45 (2))** are required to allow persons with disabilities to be accompanied by their Service Animal in all areas open to the public. A Medical Letter from a [**Regulated Health Professional**](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/r16165) **(O. Reg. 165/16 s. 80.45 (4)b)** is the official documentation that satisfies this legal requirement.

### Summary of Ontario Legal Framework:

* [**Identification & Documentation:**](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/r16165) Service animals are **not** legally required to wear a vest or harness. Under **O. Reg. 165/16, s. 80.45 (4)a**, a Medical Letter from a Regulated Health Professional is the definitive and sole proof required.

* [**General Public Access:**](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/r16165) Per **O. Reg. 165/16, s. 80.47 (2)**, obligated organizations shall ensure that a person with a disability is permitted to enter the premises with their service animal and keep the animal with them.

* [**Food Establishments:**](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/170493#BK17) Under the **_Health Protection and Promotion Act_ (O. Reg. 493/17, s. 14)**, service animals are expressly exempt from rules prohibiting animals in areas where food is served, sold, or offered for sale.

* [**Privacy Rights:**](https://www.canlii.org/en/on/onhrt/doc/2010/2010hrto61/2010hrto61.html) The **_Ontario Human Rights Code_** prohibits businesses from demanding disclosure of a person's medical diagnosis or proof of the animal's training. (*Allarie v. Rouble, 2010 HRTO 61*).

Thank you for [ensuring your staff is aware of these guidelines](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/r16165) **(O. Reg. 165/16 s. 80.49 (1))** to maintain compliance with Ontario's accessibility and human rights laws.
