# Section 3: Obtaining Your Medical Documentation

A medical letter is the **sole legal requirement** in Ontario to verify a service animal when it is not wearing a vest. This section provides the tools to secure this document efficiently.

### Contents:
* **Request Template:** A professional email to send to your doctor or nurse practitioner.
* **Letter Template:** A ready-to-use draft for your health professional to sign, ensuring all AODA-required language is present.

*Note: You do not need to disclose your specific diagnosis.*
