# Section 1: Legal Framework (Ontario)

This section provides a comprehensive breakdown of the laws protecting **Service Animal Users** in Ontario. 

### Quick Reference:
* **Identification:** Only a medical letter is required if the animal is not wearing a vest (**O. Reg. 165/16, s. 80.45 (4)b**).
* **Food Areas:** Access is explicitly protected in restaurants and grocery stores (**O. Reg. 493.17, s. 14 (2)**).
* **Civil Rights:** Denial of access is a violation of the _Ontario Human Rights Code_. Fines up to $25,000. (**R.S.O. 1990, c. H.19 s. 46.2 (1)**).
