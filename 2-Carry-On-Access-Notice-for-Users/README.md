# Section 2: Carry-On Access Notice

This section is your primary "Digital Shield!" It displays well on a smartphone screen to immediately inform business owners and security personnel of their legal obligations.

### How to use, when there is hesitation to let you in:
1. **Open the sub-page** "Service Animal Access Notice".
2. **Present your phone** to the staff member or manager.
3. **Point to the specific regulation** (e.g., Food Premises) if they cite health codes as a reason for denial.

### Key Legal Citations included in this notice:
* **O. Reg. 165/16 s. 80.45 (2):** Requirement to allow service animals.
* **O. Reg. 165/16 s. 80.45 (4)b:** Recognition of the Medical Letter.
* **O. Reg. 493/17 s. 14:** Explicit exemption for food service areas.
