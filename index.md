# Ontario Service Animal Access Guide
Legal Resource & Advocacy Tool (AODA / IASR)

This guide provides the necessary legal references to ensure universal access for Service Animal users in Ontario.

## Part 1: Legal Framework & Access Rights
Understanding business obligations and user rights.

*Access Rights Myths: Law prevalence over internal policies and access to areas where food is served.
*Legal Definition: Identification criteria for Service Animals (invisible disabilities and no mandatory certification).
*Verification Protocols: The Medical Letter as the sole legally required proof.
*Legal References: Case law and privacy rights regarding the user's disability.

## Part 2: Carry-On Access Notice
A concise, one-page legislative summary designed for immediate reading during an access dispute. 
Carry it on you along with your Medical Letter!

## Part 3: Medical Documentation
Procedures and templates for obtaining an AODA-compliant Medical Letter from a Health Professional.

## Part 4: Complaint Procedures
Step-by-step actions following discrimination: Management email/letter templates and real-world legal consequences.
